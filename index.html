Rides across West Palm Beach, with real GPS

One person requests a ride. The other accepts it and drives. The rider watches the car move on the map, and both phones follow the trip to the receipt.

Testing with someone else

  1. Both of you open this app on your phones.
  2. Allow location when your phone asks.
  3. One taps Ride, the other taps Drive.

Testing at home, not driving? The driver can turn on Test drive to move the car on the map automatically.
