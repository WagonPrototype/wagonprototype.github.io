WPB Rides privacy

Last updated October 9, 2026. WPB Rides is a test version of a rideshare app for West Palm Beach. It is used by a small group of testers and does not take payments.

What the app collects

  • Your location, while the app is open on your screen. Riders' locations are used for pickup. Drivers' locations are shared while they are online, and the path driven during a trip is saved with the ride.
  • Addresses you search for, which are sent to Google to find matching places.
  • What you type in: your first name, and for drivers, the car, plate and an optional photo.
  • Ride details: pickup, destination, fare shown, times, distance and rating.
  • A random app ID for your phone, so the app can tell your rides apart from other people's. You don't create an account.

Who can see it

Drivers who are online can see open ride requests, including the rider's first name and pickup spot. Once a driver accepts a ride, that rider sees the driver's name, photo, car, plate and live location until the trip ends. People using the app can see which drivers are online and roughly where they are.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase, in the United States. Maps, address search and road routes are provided by Google Maps Platform, under Google's privacy policy. The app does not sell your data, show ads, or share data with anyone else.

Removing your data

To have your rides and driver profile deleted, contact the developer using the email address on the app's Google Play page.
